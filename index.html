BE A RESPONSIBLE CITIZEN

Waste Management Guide

Proper waste segregation makes recycling easier and reduces the amount of waste sent to landfills.

🍃

Wet / Organic Waste

Food scraps, fruit peels, vegetable waste and suitable garden waste.

Action: Compost when possible.
📦

Dry Recyclable Waste

Clean paper, cardboard, metal cans and accepted plastic packaging.

Action: Keep it clean and sort it.
🔋

Special / Hazardous Waste

Batteries, e-waste, chemicals and certain household hazardous products.

Action: Use authorized collection points.

Note: Follow your local waste collection rules. Not all plastics or paper products are recyclable.

LIVE A LITTLE GREENER

Simple Eco-Friendly Practices

🛍️

Reduce Plastic

Carry reusable bags and water bottles instead of choosing single-use alternatives.

🚲

Travel Green

Walk, cycle, carpool or use public transport whenever practical.

⚡

Save Energy

Switch off unused lights and appliances. Choose energy-efficient devices.

🌱

Grow Plants

Plant native species and care for local greenery and biodiversity.

🚰

Conserve Water

Fix leaks, turn off taps when not needed, and avoid wasting clean water.

🧴

Reuse Products

Repair, refill, donate and reuse items before buying new ones.
